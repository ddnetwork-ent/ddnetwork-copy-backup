Care Instructions

Cleaning:
Clean your leather card holder regularly with a soft, dry cloth. For deeper cleaning, use a specialized leather cleaner applied sparingly with a soft cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals and excessive water.

Storage:
Store your card holder in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Use the provided dust bag when not in use to protect the leather. Avoid overfilling to maintain the shape and integrity of the product.
